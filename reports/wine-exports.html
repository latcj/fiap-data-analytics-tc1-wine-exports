Brazilian wine exports, 2009–2023¶

FIAP postgraduate program in Data Analytics · Tech Challenge 1

Which destinations bought the most Brazilian wine, and where did sales grow most quickly? This study compares annual export revenue in US dollars, then extrapolates the historical trend for selected markets.

Data and scope¶

Embrapa’s VitiBrasil series covers 1970–2023. The production and domestic-sales tables cover Rio Grande do Sul, while the export table records sales from Brazil by destination in kilograms and US dollars. The regional tables also use different product categories. We use the national export table for all destination comparisons and retain the other two tables for context. Embrapa VitiBrasil

1. Leading export destinations¶

Paraguay ranked first at $42.86 million over the 15 years, Russia second at $23.15 million and the United States third at $9.31 million. A cumulative ranking gives the same weight to a dollar earned in any year. The annual series shows how differently those totals were reached.

2. Annual sales in the six largest markets¶

Russia’s sales were concentrated in 2012–2014. Paraguay’s sales continued to grow and reached $5.52 million in 2023. The linear scale shows the size of these flows. The following log-scale comparison makes changes in smaller markets easier to see.

Conclusion¶

Paraguay stands out for both scale and sustained sales. Russia’s high historical total reflects a short-lived peak. Haiti and Uruguay warrant closer study because the high growth rates started from small bases. In the United States and China, the historical trend raises a different question: whether particular products or price segments performed better than the aggregate. The export table cannot answer that product-level question.
